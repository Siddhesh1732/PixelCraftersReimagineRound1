FAQs

What is Maruti Suzuki Subscribe?

expand_more

It's a car Subscription service. With Maruti Suzuki Subscribe, you can opt for any of the Maruti Suzuki cars available in the Subscription portfolio. Choose from a flexible tenure and pay only a fixed all-inclusive monthly fee. This fee includes the cost of car usage, maintenance, insurance, and service.

Am I eligible for Maruti Suzuki Subscribe?

expand_more

Be a resident of India
Be 21 years old or above (Cut off age may vary across Subscription Partners)
Own a Valid Driver's License
Have a Credit score of 650 and above (Cut off score may vary across Subscription Partners)

Can I select the colour of my car?

expand_more

Maruti Suzuki Subscribe offers multiple colour options to choose from.

Can I use the car for rideshare services?

expand_more

No, the car cannot be used for any commercial purposes.

Can I make modifications or alterations to the car?

expand_more

No, you cannot make any modifications or alterations to the car provided to you. However, you can fix detachable accessories which can be easily removed, as long as the warranty of the car does not get affected.
